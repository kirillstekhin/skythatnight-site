Personalised star map gift UK: how it works, what’s printed, delivery by Christmas

A personalised star map is a print of the night sky as it was above one place at one moment — a wedding, a first date, the night someone was born — with your own words beneath it. Here is how ours are made, what ends up on the paper, and when to order if it is a Christmas gift.

How it works

  1. Choose the moment. Enter the date, the time and the place — any town or village worldwide. The map on the page is drawn from your details as you type.
  2. Write your words. A title line of up to 40 characters, such as names or “The night we met”. The place and date are printed beneath it.
  3. Choose the look. A colour theme, a size and a finish — print only or a classic frame — then pay securely.
  4. Check the preview. Within 24 hours of your payment we email you the finished file, exactly as it will be printed. If something needs changing, reply and we send a new preview; if it looks right, you don't need to do anything.
  5. Printed and delivered. Each piece is printed to order by our fine-art print partner, dispatched within 3–6 working days and delivered by tracked courier — free to any UK or US address.

What's printed

  • The stars as they were. Positions are calculated for your date, time and place from a catalogue based on the Hipparcos space-telescope measurements, moved to your date. We draw every star down to about the faintest a sharp eye can see under a dark sky — roughly 6,000 above the horizon on any one night.
  • Constellations and the Milky Way. The traditional constellation figures are drawn to help you find your way; they are not official boundaries.
  • The Moon's phase at that moment, shown beneath the map.
  • Your words, with the place and the date.
  • Not shown: planets, weather and light pollution, buildings or hills — the sky is drawn as if the night were clear and dark. More in How accurate is a star map?

If something isn't right

Each map is personalised, so it is exempt from the usual change-of-mind return — please check names, date, time and place before you order. If it arrives damaged, faulty or not as previewed, we reprint it free or refund it in full. Details: Delivery & Returns.

Will a star map arrive in time for Christmas in the UK?

Is it the real sky?

Yes. Star positions are calculated for the date, time and place you enter, so two different nights give two different maps.
